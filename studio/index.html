EPISODE V2 · FORTY DAYS A YEAR

Our feeds take about 40 full days a year. Meta's own annual report says its results depend on "engaging active users". Sixteen cents a day each, times 3.5 billion. A sleep study, checked honestly. Step one: tonight, the phone sleeps outside your room. Stop scrolling. Go to sleep.
