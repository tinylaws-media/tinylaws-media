Not Just You, the studio

The code that makes each 61–66 second episode: a bedroom at night, one warm lamp, Lumen the moth, sourced captions and a calm voice. Everything is generated; nothing to license.

The look

Cold blues everywhere except the lamp, which stands for help and other people. Lumen never speaks: he circles the phone's light, and settles by the lamp when the episode offers a way out. Fixed rain and grain, slow camera, no flashes, no shakes. Type is Instrument Serif, roman and italic.

Rules that held up in review

  • Sensation from frame 0, reframe with one sourced number by about 10 s, mechanism, one path for you and one together, “it's not just you” looping into the first line.
  • Every figure carries its source under the caption; “a link, not proof” for surveys, “predicts, not proves” for long studies; no invented chart data.
  • Captions mirror the voice, five words a line, two lines, nothing past x 900.
  • Helpline on screen when the topic touches distress; no names, no titles, no treatment advice.
  • Every episode goes through an independent reviewer before anyone sees it.
