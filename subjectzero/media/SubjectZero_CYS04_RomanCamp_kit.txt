THE NUMBERS AND WHERE THEY COME FROM (for replies)
• Vindolanda: Roman fort just south of Hadrian's Wall (Northumberland), first fort c. AD 85; the wall came later (120s).
• "Carry about 20 kg, march 30 km in just over 6 hours": Vegetius (c. AD 400) says recruits marched 20 Roman miles (29.6 km) in 5 summer hours (about 75 min each) and carried "not less than 60 pounds" (19.7 kg).
• Sick list: Tab. Vindol. 154, strength report of the 1st Cohort of Tungrians: 296 present, 31 unfit (15 sick, 6 wounded, 10 with sore eyes).
• Socks, sandals, 2 pairs of underpants: Tab. Vindol. 346. "My fellow-soldiers have no beer. Please order some to be sent": Tab. Vindol. 628 (Masclus, a decurion).
• Latrines: seats side by side, no cubicles, probably a shared sponge on a stick (English Heritage, Housesteads, the next fort on the Wall).
• 25 years: the minimum term for auxiliary soldiers (citizenship at discharge, on a bronze diploma). "If you make it": many did not, but I give no survival odds because I could not source a figure.
• The tablets: first found 1973, more than 1,700 so far. Tab. Vindol. 291, Claudia Severa's birthday invitation (11 Sept), with lines in her own hand: almost certainly the earliest known Latin written by a woman.
