THE NUMBERS AND WHERE THEY COME FROM (for replies)
• Point Nemo (48°52.6′S 123°23.6′W) is 2,688 km from the nearest land: Ducie Island, Motu Nui (off Easter Island) and Maher Island (off Antarctica). Guinness World Records.
• The ISS orbits about 400 km up; when it passes over, its crew can be the nearest humans. BBC Sky at Night.
• Sea surface around 7 °C on average (9 °C measured during a 2024 visit). inaccessibility.net.
• Cold water: gasping in the first minute, loss of useful muscle control after about 10 minutes (the 1-10-1 rule, US National Weather Service). Even staying afloat (a life jacket, say), expected survival in 5–10 °C water is about 1–3 hours without a survival suit (standard immersion chart; it varies a lot from person to person).
• 18 days: 2,688 km at the 1,500 m freestyle world-record pace (14:30.67, Bobby Finke, 2024), no stop, no current. Our arithmetic.
• Rubber ducks: 28,800 bath toys fell into the Pacific in January 1992; some drifted for years, as far as the Atlantic.
• At least 264 spacecraft were sunk in the area between 1971 and 2016 (Mir and six Salyut stations among them); the ISS is planned to come down there around 2030.
• About 4 km of water under her: the ocean floor around Point Nemo is about 4 km down. inaccessibility.net.
