# Could you survive? #05 — Mars, no suit: fact sheet (checked 30 Oct 2026)

Each number used in the episode, with its source. "Spoken" is what the narration says; "screen" what the HUD and the
captions show. The place: **the surface of Mars** at an ordinary spot (average surface pressure), in daylight. Main
assumption of the episode: she spawns **unprotected** (a hoodie, no suit, no oxygen). At Mars's pressure the body
behaves as in a near-vacuum, so the answer comes from human vacuum accidents and animal vacuum studies. It is an
**estimate with a wide range**: about 15 s of consciousness (reports run from about 9–12 s to 15 s), and about 90 s
within which animals reliably recovered when re-pressurised (longer exposures were often fatal). The episode never
shows harm: she keeps reacting, the lab keeps noting.

Not used, or left out on purpose:
- **Freezing.** Popular sources disagree on how fast an unprotected body would cool in thin, cold air (Sky at Night
  says "severe hypothermia in minutes" for an unheated astronaut); it doesn't change the answer (oxygen does), so the
  episode doesn't claim it.
- **"Your blood boils."** Blood is held under the body's own pressure; the boiling shown is the exposed water (tongue,
  eyes, skin). The episode only says "the water on your tongue".
- **Radiation, dust toxicity (perchlorates).** Real, but slow next to 15 seconds; not used.
- **The dog deaths** in the 1965 study are not shown or said; the voice says only the rescue window.

Running gags (not facts): her NOPE, the lab, the duck ("It doesn't breathe": a rubber duck has no lungs), the respawn.
