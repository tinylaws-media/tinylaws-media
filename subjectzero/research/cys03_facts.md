# Could you survive? #03 — Death Valley in July: fact sheet (checked 29 Oct 2026)

Each number used in the episode, with its source. "Spoken" is what the narration says; "screen" what the HUD and the
captions show. Main assumption of the episode: she **spawns** on the Badwater salt flat on a July day with **one litre
of water**, and the survival time is the classic military estimate for a person who **rests in the shade** on a day
whose shade maximum is about 120 °F (49 °C). The July average high at Furnace Creek is 116 °F, so 120 °F is a hot but
ordinary July day. It is an estimate from a 1940s field study, not a law; people vary a lot.

Not used, or left out on purpose: heat deaths in the park (no graphic content, and the park's yearly count varies);
"you sweat X litres an hour" (FM 21-76 gives 2.5–3.5 L/h "in an extreme climate", which looks high next to sports
science, so we use the army's drinking guideline instead); frying an egg on the ground (the park asks people not to);
the 1913 record's exact dispute details (only "officially" in the voice). The real people of 1849 are mentioned,
never drawn.

Review (29 Oct 2026, independent agent): every claim OK; applied: "supposedly" for the quote, and the 120 °F shade
assumption stated in the caption. Note: a 116 °F average July day sits between the 110 °F (3 days) and 120 °F
(2 days) rows; "about two days" is the hotter-day value.
