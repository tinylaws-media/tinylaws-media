# Could you survive? #01 — Point Nemo: fact sheet (checked 28 Oct 2026)

Each number used in the episode, with its source. "Spoken" is what the narration says; "screen" what the HUD shows.

| Claim | Spoken / screen | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Point Nemo, the oceanic pole of inaccessibility, 48.87°S 123.39°W (48°52.6′S 123°23.6′W) | "the farthest point from land" | [Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/66641-remotest-spot-from-land); [inaccessibility.net](https://inaccessibility.net/nemo/) | High |
| 2,688 km (1,670 mi) from the nearest land | "2,688 kilometers" · screen 2,688 km | Guinness; [Sky at Night](https://www.skyatnightmagazine.com/earth-from-space/point-nemo); [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/international-space-station/meet-point-nemo-where-the-international-space-station-will-die-in-2030) | High |
| Nearest land: Ducie Island (Pitcairn Islands, north), Motu Nui (islet off Easter Island, north-east), Maher Island (off Antarctica, south), roughly equidistant | "Two tiny islands… and one off Antarctica" · map labels | Guinness | High |
| When the ISS passes over, the nearest humans can be its astronauts, about 400 km up | "Sometimes the nearest humans are in space" · screen ↑ 400 km | Sky at Night | High (it is "sometimes": only during a pass; the ISS's 51.6° orbit reaches 48.9°S) |
| Sea surface ≈ 7 °C on average (9 °C measured on the 20 Mar 2024 visit) | "around seven degrees" · screen 7 °C / 45 °F | inaccessibility.net (expedition notes) | Medium: one source; seasonal range not found. Say "around" |
| Ocean depth ≈ 4 km | screen only, optional | inaccessibility.net | Medium |
| Waves 2.5–3 m on the swim day; 6–7 m on the way out; 11 m forecast | not used (could justify a storm beat later) | inaccessibility.net | Medium |
| Cold shock: involuntary gasp in the first minute; ~10 min until loss of muscle control; ~1 h to hypothermia (1-10-1, Giesbrecht) | "In the first minute, the cold makes you gasp" · "after about ten minutes, your arms stop working" | [US National Weather Service, cold water safety (archived)](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20201218024208/https://www.weather.gov/safety/coldwater); [Canadian Safe Boating Council](https://csbc.ca/1-10-1-principle/) | High |
| Water 5–10 °C (40–50 °F): exhaustion or unconsciousness 30–60 min, expected survival 1–3 h | "you'd last about one to three hours" · screen 1–3 h | Standard immersion chart reproduced by [boating.ncf.ca](https://boating.ncf.ca/hypothermia.html), [aceboater](https://aceboater.com/en-us/hypothermia-water-temperature-chart) (chart usually credited to the US Coast Guard / US SAR Task Force) | Medium-high: it is an estimate, without a survival suit, varies a lot by person; say "about" |
| Men's 1,500 m freestyle world record 14:30.67 (Bobby Finke, 4 Aug 2024) = 1.72 m/s = 6.2 km/h → 2,688 km nonstop = 433 h ≈ 18 days | "Even at Olympic world-record pace, without stopping… eighteen days" | [World Aquatics](https://www.worldaquatics.com/news/4075653/usa-bobby-finke-men-1500m-freestyle-world-record-wr-paris-2024-olympic-games) + our arithmetic (2688 / 6.202 = 433.4 h = 18.06 days) | High (hypothesis stated: no rest, no current) |
| Friendly Floatees: 28,800 bath toys lost in the North Pacific on 10 Jan 1992; some drifted over 27,000 km, years in Arctic ice, landfalls up to 2007 | "Bath toys lost at sea in 1992 drifted for years" | [Wikipedia, Friendly Floatees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friendly_Floatees); [Futility Closet](https://www.futilitycloset.com/2019/12/06/the-friendly-floatees/) | High |
| Spacecraft cemetery around Point Nemo: at least 264 spacecraft disposed there 1971–2016 (Mir, six Salyut stations) | "More than two hundred sixty" | [Wikipedia, Spacecraft cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spacecraft_cemetery) (citing Popular Science) | High (count dates from 2016; it is higher now) |
| ISS to be deorbited around 2030 by SpaceX's US Deorbit Vehicle into a remote area of the South Pacific (the cemetery) | "Next in line, around 2030: that space station" | Space.com ("toward the end of 2030"); [Business Today, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/photo/what-will-happen-to-the-international-space-station-in-2030-538991-2026-06-24) | High for "around 2030"; exact date not fixed |
| Drinking seawater dehydrates: kidneys can only make urine less salty than seawater | (cut from the demo for time) | [NOAA Ocean Service](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/drinksw.html) | High |

Not used, not verified well enough: "almost no ships ever pass" (no solid source), "the most lifeless spot in the ocean" (the South Pacific Gyre desert is centred further north; Point Nemo is at its southern edge at best).
